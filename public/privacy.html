Stocker — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Introduction

Stocker is operated independently. Respecting user privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information is processed when accessing or using Stocker ("the Service").

Information Processing

Stocker is designed to minimize data collection:

  • Information You Provide: If you contact support or submit inquiries, the email address and message details provided will be used solely to respond to you.
  • Account Information: If an account is created, necessary authentication details (such as email address and password credentials) are processed to secure your login.

What Is NOT Collected or Tracked

To provide full transparency, Stocker explicitly does not do any of the following:

  • No Tracking Cookies: Stocker does not use tracking, advertising, or third-party cookies.
  • No Analytics or Behavioral Tracking: Stocker does not log individual page visits, track browsing activity, or build behavioral profiles.
  • No Data Sales: Personal data is never sold, rented, or commercialized.

Data Security

Standard administrative and technical measures are maintained to protect account credentials and support communications. However, no internet-based transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

If the Service links to external websites or third-party resources, this Privacy Policy does not apply to those outside services. Reviewing their respective privacy policies is recommended.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect service adjustments. The revision date at the top of this page indicates when the latest changes took effect.

Contact

For questions or privacy inquiries regarding Stocker, send an email to privacy@stocker.example.
